📜 What is My Will?

A personal app that helps you write your will and important messages, and keep them updated. All data is stored locally on your browser and never sent to any external server.

🔒 Privacy & Security

  • ✅ Data is stored only on your device (Local Storage).
  • ✅ We do not use unnecessary cookies.
  • ✅ We do not send any data to external parties.
  • ✅ The app works offline after initial load.

⚖️ Legal Disclaimer

My Will is a personal organization and documentation tool only. It does not constitute a legally binding will or official document. For a legally valid will, please consult a lawyer or competent authority in your jurisdiction.
